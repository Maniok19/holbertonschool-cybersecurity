Section 4.2: Prohibited Activities

Users shall not engage in any activity that compromises the security, integrity, or performance of BioHealth's network, systems, or data. Specifically, the following activities are strictly prohibited:

    Cryptocurrency Mining: Users are strictly prohibited from installing, executing, or operating any cryptocurrency mining software, scripts, or hardware on BioHealth premises, servers, workstations, or networks. Utilizing company computing resources for the mining or generation of digital currencies is a severe violation of this policy.

    Unauthorized Software Installation: Users are strictly prohibited from downloading, installing, or executing any unauthorized software, applications, or code on company devices. All software installations must be pre-approved by the IT Security department and comply with the company's software asset management policy.

Consequences of Violation: Violation of these provisions constitutes a serious breach of this Acceptable Use Policy. Depending on the severity and intent of the violation, consequences may include immediate revocation of system access, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and/or legal action, including referral to law enforcement and financial restitution for damages incurred by BioHealth.
